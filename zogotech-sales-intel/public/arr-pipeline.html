Why This Report Exists. Pipedrive stores ARR on each deal but totals every list, board and forecast at contract value, which is roughly three times larger. A dashboard card reading $2.78M ARR that opened straight into a Pipedrive view reading $7.83M would look broken. Every ARR figure on the dashboard now opens here, where the number on the card is the number on the page. The green links go through to the matching Pipedrive list when you want to work the records themselves.
