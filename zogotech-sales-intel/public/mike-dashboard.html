Opens in Pipedrive
Deal counts, stage membership and lost reasons. Nothing on these three depends on ARR, so Pipedrive reproduces them exactly and these open straight into the app.
Why the Split. A card reading $2.78M ARR that opened into a Pipedrive view reading $7.83M would look broken to anyone who clicked it. So any card carrying a revenue figure now opens a report page where the headline matches, and each of those pages carries a green link through to the matching Pipedrive list when you want to work the records. Counts and reasons are unambiguous, so those cards still go straight to Pipedrive.

ARR and TCV. Every revenue card defaults to recurring revenue. The toggle switches all of them to total contract value, which folds in multi-year terms and one-time services. Quota and attainment are always ARR, so the attainment card does not move. The stage split came out of the pull in ARR only and is marked accordingly.
