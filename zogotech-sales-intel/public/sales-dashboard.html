What This Can and Cannot See. Pipedrive records that a deal changed and when, and it exposes the moments that matter on their own fields: created, stage moved, won and lost. It does not expose a field-by-field history through the connector, so a row marked Edited means the record was written to that day without saying which field moved. Everything decisive is caught; small edits are counted but not described.

Changed Is Not the Same as Worked. Fifteen deals changed. Only five of them, carrying $523,000, had a call, meeting or email logged against them. The other ten were edited without any selling activity recorded: Missouri Western, Northland Pioneer, Highland, KCKCC, Salt Lake, Denver, Eastern Wyoming, Morton, Harrisburg and the FoundationCCC RFP that was closed out. Some of those edits will be admin, but a $158,500 deal like Salt Lake changing with nothing logged is worth a question.

Four Contract Values Moved Last Week. City College of San Francisco, Morton College, Wallace State Hanceville and Harrisburg Area all had their contract value changed. Harrisburg moved the most, from $250,000 down to $110,000. ARR is unchanged on all four, so nothing in the quota reporting shifts. Every report here now carries the current figures, but only because they were pulled again by hand. That is the clearest argument for a refresh button rather than a snapshot.
