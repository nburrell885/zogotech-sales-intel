About the 19 April Bulk Load. Twelve leads were created on a single day, 19 April 2026, all owned by Rebecca. That is a bulk entry, not a day of prospecting, and it flattens every other week on the chart when included. The report excludes it by default. Switch the toggle at the top to see the raw figures.

Where the ARR Comes From. A lead carries no value in Pipedrive, so ARR appears only once the lead has been converted into a deal, and it is that deal’s recurring revenue. The Deal Now column says whether that deal is still open, won, or since lost, so a large figure against a lost deal is visible rather than flattering. Two converted leads have deals with no ARR recorded yet and show as not set.

What Counts as a Lead Here. Every row in the Pipedrive leads inbox, counted on its created date and attributed to its owner. Converted leads stay in the count for the period they were set, so this measures activity going in rather than outcomes coming out. Lead value is not tracked, so this report counts leads and does not weight them.
