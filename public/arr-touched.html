What Counts as Meaningful. Calls, meetings, discovery calls, demos, emails, and tasks that a rep logged by hand. Marketing automation is excluded: newsletter sends, opens, clicks, unsubscribes, and website visit tracking all fire automatically and would swamp the count without telling you anything about selling effort.

How ARR Touched Is Calculated. Every meaningful activity is traced to its linked deal, and that deal’s ARR is counted once for the week. Working the same deal four times does not multiply the number. Activities linked only to a person or organization with no deal behind them are still listed, but they carry no ARR and are marked as prospecting.
